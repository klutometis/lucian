# Glossary of Key Terms

## Philosophical / Cynic Vocabulary

- **κύων / κυνικός** (kyon / kynikos): "dog" / "cynic." Menippus is called
  a "dog" (κύνα) by Diogenes — a term of *honor* among Cynics. Render as
  "dog" with the clear sense that it's a compliment: "you old dog."

- **παρρησία** (parrhesia): frank speech, saying what you think without
  fear. Hermes lets Menippus keep it at the end of Dialogue 10. Render
  as "the freedom to say whatever he wants" or "his big mouth" depending
  on tone.

- **αὐτάρκεια** (autarkeia): self-sufficiency. The Cynic ideal.
  "Needing nothing" works.

- **τῦφος** (typhos): literally smoke/vapor; figuratively: pomposity,
  self-importance, inflated ego. Render as "hot air," "the ego," "the
  puffery."

## Underworld Bureaucracy

- **πορθμεῖα** (porthmeia): the ferry fee (Charon's obol). "The fare."

- **δικαστήριον** (dikasterion): the judgment court in the underworld.
  "The tribunal."

- **Μοῖρα / Κλωθώ** (Moira / Clotho): Fate / the spinner of life-threads.
  "Fate" is fine; Clotho can stay as Clotho.

## Social Types (recurring character archetypes)

- **κόλαξ** (kolax): flatterer, legacy-hunter, sycophant. These are the
  people who fawn on rich old men hoping to inherit. Render as "legacy-
  hunter" or "flatterer" or "suck-up" depending on context.

- **ἄτεκνος** (ateknos): childless. Being childless is what makes a rich
  old man a target for legacy-hunters. Keep the word close: "no kids,"
  "died childless."

- **σατράπης** (satrapes): Persian provincial governor. Shorthand for
  "eastern potentate." "Satrap" is fine; audience will get it.
