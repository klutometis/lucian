## The Obol

Charon's ferry fee. Keep "obol" — it's funnier than "coin" and the audience
will get it from context. Can gloss once on first use: "the obol — the fare."

## Underworld Geography

- ᾅδης / Hades = "the underworld" or just "down here"
- Λήθης = "the Lethe" (river of forgetting)
- Πυριφλεγέθων = "the Pyriphlegethon" (OK to skip on first use, gloss as
  "the fire river" if needed)
- Ἠλύσιον πεδίον = "the Elysian Fields"

## Cynics

Diogenes, Menippus, Crates, Antisthenes are Cynics. They travel light,
own nothing, laugh at everyone. This is their entire personality and they
are fine with it. Translate their lines with maximum philosophical ease.

## Recurring Joke

The wealthy dead keep mourning their treasure, kingdoms, good looks.
The Cynics find this hilarious. This is the recurring bit — play it up.
