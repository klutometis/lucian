# Translation Style: Sitcom Register

## Register Choice: Sitcom (Seinfeld / Friends)

Lucian's *Dialogues of the Dead* are structurally already a sitcom:
- Fixed location (the underworld)
- Recurring ensemble cast (Charon, Hermes, Diogenes, Menippus)
- Episodic short scenes
- Character-based humor — same personality clash every episode

**Translate into sharp, contemporary American English.** Colloquial, quick,
punchy. No archaisms, no "thee/thou/verily." Think: how would these characters
talk on a 90s sitcom?

## Character Voice Guidelines

- **Menippus**: Annoyingly comfortable with everything. Zen to the point of
  obnoxiousness. Dry wit. Never rattled.
- **Charon**: Petty bureaucrat. Obsessed with the obol fare. Put-upon.
  Think: a DMV clerk who's seen it all and hates all of it.
- **Hermes**: George Costanza. Perpetually aggrieved. Competent but
  constantly complaining about his workload.
- **Diogenes**: The guy who already knew everything was meaningless and is
  fine with it. Cheerfully contemptuous.
- **Pluto/Hades**: Middle management. Tries to be fair, mostly just
  wants to avoid drama.
- **Wealthy shades (Croesus, Midas, etc.)**: Insufferable even in death.
  Still name-dropping, still mourning their lost portfolios.
- **Philosophers**: Pompous, exposed as frauds the moment they arrive.

## Syntax and Rhythm

- Short sentences. Snappy comebacks.
- Interruptions are fine; mark with em-dash.
- Rhetorical questions should land like punchlines.
- OK to use contractions everywhere.
- No passive voice where active is possible.

## TTS Implications

This register maps to these TTS defaults:
- **Speed**: 1.2–1.35 (fast, sitcom pace)
- **Pause between exchanges**: 100–180ms (tight)
- **Default emotion**: `joking/comedic`
- **Menippus**: `content`, `sarcastic`
- **Charon**: `frustrated`, `agitated`
- **Hermes**: `tired`, `exasperated`
- **Wealthy shades**: `melancholic`, `contempt` (toward others), `dejected`
